    Privacy

    2DayMovie has no accounts, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies of its own: the site itself sets no cookies. Your sound, volume, brightness and size choices for the header video are kept only in your own browser (localStorage, key tdm.hero.v2) and are never sent to us. Titles you save to My List are kept the same way, as a list of video ids under tdm.mylist.v1; clearing your browser data removes them. Videos are YouTube embeds from youtube-nocookie.com, YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. The trailers in the header play automatically, muted, as a live background, so YouTube's player loads as soon as you open the site. YouTube may set its own cookies and storage when trailers or videos play, under Google's Privacy Policy; we do not read or control them. Thumbnails come from YouTube's image server, i.ytimg.com.
